| Chromatic Chord Type | What to look for | Chord Labels |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Secondary Dominant | QUALITY—M triad or Mm7 | V/, V7/ |
| Secondary Diminished | QUALITY— ∘ , ∘ 7 , ø ø 7 | vii vii ∘ /, viiø vii ø 7 /, vii vii ∘ 7 / |
| Mode Mixture | ACCIDENTALS—lowered notes | ii ii ∘ , iiø ii ø 7 , iv iv , vii vii ∘ 7 , ♭VI VI , etc. |
| Neapolitan | ♭II | N |
| Augmented Sixth Chord | ∘ 3 interval | It It + 6 , Fr Fr + 6 , Ger Ger + 6 , EnGer EnGer + 6 |
