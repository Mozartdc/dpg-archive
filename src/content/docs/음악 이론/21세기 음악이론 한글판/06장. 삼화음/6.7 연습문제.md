## 1

삼화음의 종류를 분석하고 리드 시트 기호로 적어 봄. 장삼화음, 단삼화음, 증삼화음, 감삼화음뿐 아니라 sus2와 sus4도 포함되어 있음.



<img src="/images/6.7-연습문제-0.svg" alt="삼화음 종류 분석 문제" style="max-width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 4px; display: block; margin: 10px 0;" />



<details>

<summary>정답</summary>

  1. $\text{A}^{\sharp}{}^{\circ}$ · 2. $\text{F}\text{sus4}$ · 3. $\text{B}^{\flat}{+}$ · 4. $\text{D}^{\flat}$ · 5. $\text{E}\text{sus2}$



</details>


## 2

지정된 삼화음과 서스 코드를 보표에 적어 봄.



<img src="/images/6.7-연습문제-1.svg" alt="삼화음과 서스 코드 쓰기 문제" style="max-width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 4px; display: block; margin: 10px 0;" />



<details>

<summary>정답</summary>

  1. $\text{B}\text{–}\text{D}\text{–}\text{F}^{\sharp}$ · 2. $\text{E}\text{–}\text{G}^{\sharp}\text{–}\text{B}^{\sharp}$ · 3. $\text{A}^{\flat}\text{–}\text{B}^{\flat}\text{–}\text{E}^{\flat}$ · 4. $\text{C}^{\sharp}\text{–}\text{E}^{\sharp}\text{–}\text{G}^{\sharp}$ · 5. $\text{D}\text{–}\text{G}\text{–}\text{A}$ · 6. $\text{F}\text{–}\text{A}^{\flat}\text{–}\text{C}^{\flat}$



</details>


## 3

전위된 삼화음을 슬래시 기호로 분석해 봄.



<img src="/images/6.7-연습문제-2.svg" alt="전위된 삼화음 분석 문제" style="max-width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 4px; display: block; margin: 10px 0;" />



<details>

<summary>정답</summary>

  1. $\left.\text{D}\text{m}\middle/\text{F}\right.$ · 2. $\left.\text{G}\text{m}\middle/\text{D}\right.$ · 3. $\left.\text{A}^{\sharp}{}^{\circ}\middle/\text{C}^{\sharp}\right.$ · 4. $\left.\text{F}\middle/\text{A}\right.$ · 5. $\left.\text{B}^{\flat}\middle/\text{F}\right.$ · 6. $\left.\text{C}\text{m}\middle/\text{G}\right.$



</details>


## 4

지정된 전위 삼화음을 보표에 적어 봄.



<img src="/images/6.7-연습문제-3.svg" alt="전위된 삼화음 쓰기 문제" style="max-width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 4px; display: block; margin: 10px 0;" />



<details>

<summary>정답</summary>

  낮은 음부터 1. $\text{D}\text{–}\text{G}\text{–}\text{B}$ · 2. $\text{D}\text{–}\text{F}^{\sharp}\text{–}\text{B}$ · 3. $\text{F}\text{–}\text{A}^{\flat}\text{–}\text{D}^{\flat}$ · 4. $\text{B}^{\flat}\text{–}\text{E}\text{–}\text{G}$



</details>


## 5

잘못 적힌 삼화음을 고친 뒤 리드 시트 기호를 붙여 봄. 모든 코드는 가장 낮은 음이 근음인 근음 위치임.



<img src="/images/6.7-연습문제-4.svg" alt="잘못 적힌 삼화음 고치기 문제" style="max-width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 4px; display: block; margin: 10px 0;" />



<details>

<summary>정답</summary>

  1. $\text{F}^{\sharp}\text{–}\text{A}^{\sharp}\text{–}\text{C}^{\sharp}$ · 2. $\text{G}\text{–}\text{B}^{\flat}\text{–}\text{D}^{\flat}$ · 3. $\text{A}^{\flat}\text{–}\text{C}^{\flat}\text{–}\text{E}^{\flat}$



</details>
